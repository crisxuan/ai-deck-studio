# Theme Guide

Themes should change visual language without changing content contracts.

## Themes

### consulting-clean

For executive briefings, strategy decks, investor updates, and reports.

### tech-dark

For engineering talks, architecture reviews, and developer demos.

### xiaohongshu-editorial

For mobile-first explainers and carousel-style posts.

### premium-keynote

For product launches, premium pitch decks, and showcase presentations. This theme is built for stronger typography, product mockups, data panels, market maps, and keynote-style composition layouts.

### technical-blueprint

For AI infrastructure launches, architecture keynotes, runtime systems, and engineering strategy decks. Dark blueprint surface, terminal-like labels, grid structure, and systems diagrams.

### founder-editorial

For founder pitches, investor narratives, market category creation, and high-conviction product stories. Editorial typography, hard borders, magazine-style contrast, and sharp commercial emphasis.

### executive-dashboard

For weekly business reviews, growth readouts, operating plans, and KPI-heavy executive meetings. Dense dashboard cards, status accents, compact hierarchy, and decision-oriented metrics.

## Theme Rules

- Keep contrast readable.
- Preserve layout dimensions and spacing expectations.
- Do not use decorative effects that make screenshots hard to inspect.
- Prefer a small number of strong themes over many shallow variants.
